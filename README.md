# Online-Retail-Analysis-Python
Python analysis of products from a retail store using Python, Pandas and Matplotlib.

## Key Findings
Sales trends:
- Revenue is at its highest during November, likely due to Christmas shopping
- Sales start to increase during September, while Febuary produces the least revenue
- Thursday produces the most revenue, while Saturday produces the least
- Weekends produce less revenue than weekdays

Products:
- The Regency Cakestand 3 Tier produces the most revenue
- The products that produce the most revenue are mostly decorations

Countries:
- The UK generates the most income at 85%
- The largest foreign market is Ireland

Customer segments:
- Almost two thirds (65.6%) of the revenue is from 11% of customers

## Recommendations
- Increase staff in September while sales rise and to prepare for November
- Attempt to grow foreign markets like Ireland and Netherlands
- Increase promotions during January-April since revenue is lowest
